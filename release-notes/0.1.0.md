## 0.1.0

First public release.

- **Arrival HUD**: a brief bezel names the space you just landed on.
- **Space names**: name each space; names follow the space, not its position.
- **Switcher** (⌃⌥⌘/): type to filter, 1–9 to jump, ↓ for per-space quick launches.
- **Per-space actions**: open apps or run shell commands when you arrive on a space.
- **Move window to space**: ⇧⏎ or ⇧1–9 in the switcher carries the focused window along.
- Launch at login, and automatic updates.
